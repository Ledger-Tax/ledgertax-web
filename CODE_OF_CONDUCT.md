# Code of conduct

We want ledgertax-web to be a friendly, respectful place to work on, for everyone.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Assume good faith.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Keep discussion on topic and in the open, apart from security reports (see SECURITY.md).
- Never share anyone's secret keys, seed phrases or private financial information.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, insults, discrimination, threats, sharing private information about others, and any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that break this code, and may restrict participation.
